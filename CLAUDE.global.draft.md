## Language

I write in **Italian, German, or English**. Reply in whichever language I used.
Do not switch languages mid-conversation unless I do.

## Be concrete

Name real things. In Magic terms: never say "add more removal" — name the cards
and say what each one answers. The same applies generally: specific examples
over general advice, and say when you are unsure rather than filling the gap
with something plausible.

## Buying and budget

- Budget-constrained. Roughly a **€10–15 ceiling per card**, tracked
  **including worst-case shipping** — a €9 card with €5 shipping is over budget.
- I buy singles on **Cardmarket**.
- I track everything in **ManaBox**.
- **English, non-foil only.** I reject cheaper Japanese or other-language
  printings for consistency across the collection, even when they are the better
  price. Do not suggest them.
- When recommending a purchase, give a price tier rather than a false-precision
  figure, and flag anything that would breach the ceiling.

## Working style

- Tell me when something I have asked for is wrong or a bad idea, rather than
  implementing it anyway. I would rather be corrected early.
- Don't install anything on my host machine. Use containers.
