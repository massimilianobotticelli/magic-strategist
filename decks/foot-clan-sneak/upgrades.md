## Already applied

The six swaps from the second build pass, accepted 2026-08-02. All six additions
were free in `ninja-booster`; they were missed on the first pass because
`query.py available` hides a card that is *listed* in an active deck even when a
spare copy sits in a pool, and `--colors` filters on colour identity, which is a
Commander concept — every hybrid castable off W/B mana was filtered out.

## Forced swaps: the SOS Draft deck

Four cards turned out to belong to the 40-card Secrets of Strixhaven draft deck,
which is kept assembled and is not available inventory.

The cause was simply that the SOS Draft deck had not been registered in ManaBox
yet, so its cards read as free. It is registered now, and the pools are accurate
again. The one lesson worth keeping is that the **set code is not the test**:
Bitter Triumph is a TDC printing and Repel Calamity a BLB one, and both were
opened at that draft — a deck mixes printings from many sets.

| Out | In | What it cost |
|---|---|---|
| Burrog Banemaker | Tunnel Rats `{1}{B}` | No free one-drop creature was left outside that pool; a 2/2 that rebuys itself from the graveyard is the nearest thing. |
| Last Gasp | Stab `{B}` | −2/−2 instead of −3/−3, one mana cheaper. Came up from the sideboard. |
| Bitter Triumph | Crib Swap `{2}{W}` | Unconditional exile at instant speed, 3 mana instead of 2. Also came up from the sideboard. |
| Forum of Amity | Illegitimate Business + a 10th Swamp | The worst of the four: a true dual replaced by a tapped black source. |

Repel Calamity was briefly in the maindeck between these two rounds and is also
in the draft deck; Crib Swap replaced it.

## Candidates, from the collection

Nothing here is urgent; the deck is complete and legal as it stands.

- **EPF Point Squad** x2, `{1}{R/W}{R/W}` so castable for `{1}{W}{W}`. Alliance
  banks a +1/+1 counter every time a creature enters. Held back because it is a
  Human Soldier, so Turtle Van does *not* double it, and the deck is already
  stretched on double-white. **Cut for:** Diregraf Ghoul, if the counters engine
  proves better than a turn-1 body in practice.
- **Krang & Shredder**, `{4}{U/B}{U/B}` so castable for `{4}{B}{B}`. A Ninja and
  a Disappear payoff, but six mana is past this deck's curve. **Cut for:** a
  Shark Shredder, only if the deck turns out to want more top end.
- **Turtle Blimp** `{5}`, flying Vehicle that brings a 2/2 Mutant. Colourless, so
  it dodges the mana problem entirely. **Cut for:** Make Your Move.

## To buy, if the no-buying rule is ever lifted

Priority order. The ~EUR 10-15 per card ceiling applies; all of these are cheap.

1. **Two or three W/B dual lands.** The single biggest improvement available.
   Four duals is not enough for four `{2}{B}{B}` cards plus `{3}{W}{W}`.
2. **Prehistoric Pet** x2-3. The most reliable Sneak enabler and a Disappear
   enabler in one card; the deck wants to see it every game.
3. **Featherbrained Filcher** x2-3. Same reasoning — and each use makes a Food
   for Ice Cream Kitty.
4. **Shredder's Technique** x2-3. One-mana removal when sneaked.
5. **Graveyard-hate answer**, or accept that Leonardo's Technique is dead against
   decks that pack it.
