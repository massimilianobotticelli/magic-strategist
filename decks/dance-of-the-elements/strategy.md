# Dance of the Elements — strategy

> **Draft.** Written from what is actually in the decklist, not from play
> experience. To be refined in session.

**Commander:** Ashling, the Limitless — {2}{R}, five-colour identity (BGRUW)
**Target bracket:** 2 (Core) — *provisional; this is an unmodified precon*

> Elemental permanent spells you cast from your hand gain evoke {4}.
> Whenever you sacrifice a nontoken Elemental, create a token that's a copy of
> it. The token gains haste until end of turn. At the beginning of your next end
> step, sacrifice it unless you pay {W}{U}{B}{R}{G}.

## What the deck does

An **Elemental tribal** deck built on Ashling's evoke loop. Evoking an Elemental
for {4} sacrifices it on entry, which triggers Ashling to make a hasty copy —
so an evoked creature gives its enter-the-battlefield effect twice and a body
for the turn. Cards with strong ETB effects are the point: Mulldrifter,
Shriekmaw, Ingot Chewer, Foundation Breaker, Cavalier of Thorns, Greenwarden of
Murasa.

Support falls into three groups:

1. **Elemental payoffs** — Risen Reef, Horde of Notions, Incandescent Soulstoke,
   Omnath (both), Maelstrom Wanderer, Realmwalker.
2. **Mana fixing** — the deck is five colours on a {2}{R} commander, so Primal
   Beyond, Unclaimed Territory, Secluded Courtyard, Ancient Ziggurat, Timeless
   Lotus, Chromatic Lantern and the Thriving cycle carry it.
3. **Value recursion** — Greenwarden of Murasa, Haunting Voyage, Muldrotha.

## How it wins

Board presence built from repeated ETB value, then a wide attack or a large
Omnath/Titan of Industry threat. It is a midrange value deck, not a combo deck.

## Known weak points

- Mana is the real constraint: five colours off a mono-red commander.
- Ashling is the engine; without her the deck is a pile of medium Elementals.
