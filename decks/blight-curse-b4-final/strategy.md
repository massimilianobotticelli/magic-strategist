# Blight Curse — strategy

> **Draft.** Written from what is actually in the decklist, not from play
> experience. To be refined in session.

**Commander:** Auntie Ool, Cursewretch — {1}{B}{R}{G}, Jund (BGR)
**Target bracket:** 4 (Optimized) — *provisional, confirm*

> Ward—Blight 2.
> Whenever one or more -1/-1 counters are put on a creature, draw a card if you
> control that creature. If you don't control it, its controller loses 1 life.

## What the deck does

A **-1/-1 counters** deck. Auntie Ool turns every counter placement into either
a card or a drain, so the deck wants to put -1/-1 counters on things constantly
— on opponents' creatures as removal, and on its own creatures as a draw engine.

Three overlapping jobs:

1. **Counter sources** — Hapatra, Vizier of Poisons; Blowfly Infestation;
   Soul Snuffers; Midnight Banshee; Carnifex Demon; Contagion Clasp; Contagion
   Engine; Black Sun's Zenith; Incremental Blight; The Scorpion God.
2. **Counter payoffs** — Nest of Scarabs; Flourishing Defenses; Obelisk Spider;
   Kulrath Knight; Necroskitter; Yawgmoth, Thran Physician.
3. **Removal by attrition** — the counters double as interaction, so board
   control and the engine are the same cards.

Proliferate (Contagion Clasp, Contagion Engine, Evolution Sage, Vraska,
Betrayal's Sting) reuses counters already on the board rather than spending new
cards.

## How it wins

Grinding attrition rather than a single burst: Auntie Ool draws through the
deck while opponents' boards shrink, then Grave Titan, Massacre Girl or the
Yawgmoth engine closes. Zulaport Cutthroat and Exsanguinate give a drain finish
that does not need combat.

## Known weak points

- Sensitive to graveyard hate and to enchantment removal on the payoff pieces.
- Counter payoffs are individually fragile; losing Necroskitter or Nest of
  Scarabs costs the deck a whole axis.
